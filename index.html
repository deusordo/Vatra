VATRA

A more self-sufficient way to live.

VATRA is planning a community that can provide more of its own food, water, and energy while staying connected to the wider world.

See the priorities

Priorities

  • Choose a place

    Study land, growing conditions, housing, and how to care for shared resources over time.

  • Plan the essentials

    Work through what dependable power, clean water, sanitation, and food production would take.

  • Build as neighbors

    Plan how families could share skills, maintain common spaces, and prepare for emergencies.

Where it stands

VATRA is at the planning stage. The next milestone is a small demonstration site, once land, costs, and local requirements have been studied.
