VATRA

A more self-sufficient way to live.

VATRA is planning a community that can provide more of its own food, water, and energy while staying connected to the wider world.

Explore the project See the priorities

Priorities

  • Choose a place

    Study land, growing conditions, housing, and how to care for shared resources over time.

  • Plan the essentials

    Work through what dependable power, clean water, sanitation, and food production would take.

  • Build as neighbors

    Plan how families could share skills, maintain common spaces, and prepare for emergencies.

Where it stands

VATRA is at the planning stage. A small demonstration site is a future milestone, after research into land, costs, and local requirements.

Featured project

Household systems worksheet

Starter available. Document one routine, such as tracking pantry supplies or checking shared tools. The worksheet creates a one-page record you can keep or print.

  1. List what the routine depends on, who handles it, and where instructions are kept.
  2. Ask another reader to find the instructions and name the next step from the worksheet alone.
  3. Record unclear steps or missing references and update the page.

What informs the work

FEMA’s household planning guide recommends keeping a paper copy and practicing the plan. VATRA applies that idea to routine handoffs by asking a second reader to use the worksheet. FEMA communication planning guide (PDF)

The Global Ecovillage Network’s design cards address social, cultural, economic, and ecological questions. VATRA will use those questions to compare options against local conditions and the people who would maintain them. Ecovillage Design Cards

Common questions

Can I visit VATRA or apply to live there?
VATRA is planning a future demonstration site. Public visits and applications depend on a site and an operating plan. The household worksheet is available to try today.
Can I use the worksheet in a rental or an apartment?
Yes. Pick a routine you already manage. For building systems, record where to find the owner’s or manager’s instructions.
What does self-sufficiency mean here?
The goal is to meet more needs close to home while keeping access to local services, trade, and relationships.
Where does the VATRA library fit?
NuMind plans a physical collection for VATRA and offline digital copies of editions that can be shared. See the library plan.
