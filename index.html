VATRA

A more self-sufficient way to live.

VATRA explores how a community could provide more of its own essentials while staying connected to the people and tools around it.

  • Land & stewardship

    Research suitable land, housing, growing conditions, and ways to care for shared resources.

  • Essential systems

    Plan for dependable water, energy, food, sanitation, workshops, and access to healthcare.

  • Community

    Develop a model for families and neighbors to share skills, maintain infrastructure, and prepare for emergencies.
